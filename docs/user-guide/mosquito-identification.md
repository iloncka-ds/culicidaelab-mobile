# Mosquito Identification Guide

Learn how to effectively use CulicidaeLab's AI-powered mosquito identification feature to get accurate species identification results.

## How It Works

CulicidaeLab uses advanced machine learning technology to identify mosquito species from photographs. The app analyzes visual features like body patterns, wing structure, and size proportions to determine the most likely species.

### The AI Model
- **Technology**: PyTorch Lite neural network optimized for mobile devices
- **Training Data**: Thousands of mosquito images from scientific collections
- **Accuracy**: Over 90% accuracy for the most common disease-vector species
- **Processing**: All analysis happens on your device - no internet required for identification

## Taking Effective Photos

The quality of your photo directly impacts identification accuracy. Follow these guidelines for best results:

### Lighting Conditions
- **Natural Light**: Use bright, natural daylight when possible
- **Avoid Flash**: Flash can create harsh shadows and overexpose details
- **Even Lighting**: Ensure the mosquito is evenly lit without strong shadows
- **Avoid Backlighting**: Don't photograph with bright light behind the mosquito

### Camera Distance and Focus
- **Close-up**: Get as close as possible while maintaining focus
- **Fill the Frame**: The mosquito should occupy most of the image
- **Sharp Focus**: Ensure the mosquito is in sharp focus, especially the body and wings
- **Steady Hands**: Hold the camera steady or use a tripod for sharp images

### Mosquito Positioning
- **Dorsal View**: Top-down view showing the back and wings is ideal
- **Full Body**: Include the entire mosquito from head to abdomen
- **Wings Spread**: If possible, capture with wings spread for better identification
- **Avoid Partial Views**: Don't crop off important body parts

### Background and Composition
- **Plain Background**: Use a simple, contrasting background
- **Avoid Clutter**: Remove distracting objects from the frame
- **Contrast**: Ensure good contrast between the mosquito and background
- **Stable Surface**: Place the mosquito on a stable, flat surface if possible

## Using the Identification Feature

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Open Classification Tab**
   - Tap the camera icon (📷) in the bottom navigation
   - You'll see the classification screen with action buttons

2. **Capture or Select Image**
   - **Camera**: Tap the camera button to take a new photo
   - **Gallery**: Tap the gallery button to select an existing image
   - **Tips**: Follow the photo guidelines above for best results

3. **Review Your Image**
   - The selected image will appear on screen
   - Check that the mosquito is clearly visible and in focus
   - Tap "Analyze" to start the identification process

4. **Wait for Analysis**
   - The AI model will process your image (usually 1-3 seconds)
   - A progress indicator shows the analysis is in progress
   - Processing happens entirely on your device

5. **Review Results**
   - Species name (scientific and common names)
   - Confidence score (percentage)
   - Processing time
   - Associated disease information## U

### Confidence Scores
The confidence score indicates how certain the AI model is about the identification:

- **90-100%**: Very High Confidence - Highly reliable identification
- **70-89%**: High Confidence - Reliable identification with good certainty
- **50-69%**: Moderate Confidence - Reasonable identification, consider additional verification
- **Below 50%**: Low Confidence - Uncertain identification, may need better photo

### Species Information
Each identification result includes:

- **Scientific Name**: The official taxonomic name (e.g., "Aedes aegypti")
- **Common Name**: The widely used common name (e.g., "Yellow Fever Mosquito")
- **Description**: Physical characteristics and identifying features
- **Habitat**: Where this species typically lives and breeds
- **Distribution**: Geographic regions where the species is found

### Disease Risk Information
For species that transmit diseases, you'll see:

- **Disease List**: Diseases this species can transmit
- **Risk Level**: Visual indicators of disease transmission potential
- **Prevention Tips**: How to protect yourself from this species
- **Medical Information**: Symptoms and treatment information for associated diseases

## Improving Accuracy

### When Results Seem Incorrect

If the identification doesn't seem right:

1. **Check Photo Quality**: Ensure your photo meets the guidelines above
2. **Try Different Angles**: Take additional photos from different perspectives
3. **Better Lighting**: Retake the photo with improved lighting conditions
4. **Compare Features**: Use the species gallery to compare visual features
5. **Consult Experts**: For critical identifications, consult with entomologists

### Multiple Photos
For best results, consider taking several photos:
- Different angles (top, side, close-up)
- Various lighting conditions
- Multiple specimens if available
- Compare results across photos

### Challenging Scenarios

Some situations make identification more difficult:

**Damaged Specimens**
- Missing body parts reduce accuracy
- Try to capture undamaged areas clearly
- Focus on remaining identifying features

**Similar Species**
- Some species look very similar
- Pay attention to confidence scores
- Use additional identifying features from the gallery
- Consider geographic location

**Poor Photo Conditions**
- Blurry images significantly reduce accuracy
- Dark or overexposed photos are problematic
- Motion blur from moving mosquitoes
- Retake photos when conditions improve

## Best Practices

### For Researchers and Citizen Scientists

**Documentation**
- Record location, date, and time of collection
- Note environmental conditions
- Take multiple photos of each specimen
- Keep detailed field notes

**Quality Control**
- Always review confidence scores
- Cross-reference with species gallery information
- Verify geographic distribution matches your location
- Consider seasonal activity patterns

**Data Contribution**
- Submit high-quality observations to the research database
- Include accurate location information
- Add relevant notes about behavior or habitat
- Contribute to scientific understanding

### For Public Health Awareness

**Disease Prevention Focus**
- Pay special attention to disease-vector species
- Learn to recognize dangerous species in your area
- Understand seasonal activity patterns
- Know when to seek medical attention

**Community Education**
- Share knowledge with family and community
- Report unusual species or activity patterns
- Support local mosquito control efforts
- Promote awareness of mosquito-borne diseases

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### App Performance
- **Slow Processing**: Ensure sufficient device memory is available
- **App Crashes**: Restart the app and try with a smaller image
- **Model Not Loading**: Check that you have sufficient storage space

### Photo Issues
- **Blurry Results**: Use better lighting and steady hands
- **No Detection**: Ensure the mosquito is clearly visible in the frame
- **Wrong Species**: Try different angles and lighting conditions

### Technical Problems
- **Camera Not Working**: Check camera permissions in device settings
- **Gallery Access**: Verify photo library permissions
- **Storage Issues**: Free up device storage space

For additional help, see our [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting.md) or contact support.

## Advanced Tips

### Professional Use
